Privacy Policy

Last updated August 5, 2026

Short version: we do not collect your personal info. There are no ads, no trackers, and no accounts to sign up for. Here is the longer version, in plain words.

What we collect

Nothing about you. The game does not ask for your name, email, or location, and it does not send your data to us. We do not have a server that stores your info.

Where your game saves

Your progress, coins, upgrades, and settings live on your own device. If you delete the app, that save goes with it. We never see it.

Achievements and leaderboards

If you turn on Game Center on iPhone or iPad, or Google Play Games on Android, that service handles your achievements and leaderboard scores. Those parts are run by Apple and Google and follow their own privacy rules. You can turn either one off any time in your device settings, and the game plays fine without them.

Smart bills

On newer iPhones and iPads the game can use Apple Intelligence to write funny bills based on the job you pick. That work happens right on your device. The text stays on your device and is not sent anywhere. Android uses a handwritten set of bills instead.

Kids

The game is friendly for all ages and does not knowingly collect info from anyone, including kids. There is no chat, no sharing of personal info, and no way to talk to other players.

Changes

If we ever change this policy, we will post the new version here and update the date at the top.

Questions

Reach out any time at grt.feedback@pm.me and we will get back to you.
