Privacy Policy

Last updated August 19, 2026

Short version: we do not collect your personal info. There are no ads, no trackers, no analytics, and no account to sign up for. The game has no server to send anything to. Here is the longer version, in plain words.

What we collect

Nothing about you. The game does not ask for your name, email, or location, and it does not read your contacts, photos, or files. There is no analytics kit and no crash reporter inside it. The app makes no network calls of its own — the only things it talks to are Apple’s and Google’s own services, described below, and only when you use the feature that needs them.

Where your game saves

Your progress, coins, upgrades, and settings live on your own device, in a file the game owns. If you delete the app, that save goes with it. We never see it. If you back your phone up to iCloud or to Google, the save can ride along in that backup — that backup is yours, held by Apple or Google under their rules, and we have no access to it. There is no cross-device sync, and iPhone and Android progress are entirely separate.

The one purchase

The game is free to start, and one purchase unlocks the rest of it. Apple and Google handle that payment end to end. We never see your card, your billing address, or your store account. What comes back to the game is a yes-or-no answer about whether this device’s store account owns the unlock, which is also what “Restore purchases” re-checks. That answer is kept on your device.

Achievements and leaderboards

If you turn on Game Center on iPhone or iPad, or Google Play Games on Android, that service handles your achievements and leaderboard scores. Those parts are run by Apple and Google, use the account you already have with them, and follow their own privacy rules rather than this one. You can turn either off any time in your device settings, and the game plays fine without them. Nothing else about you is attached to a score.

Bill reminders

The game can remind you that a bill is coming due. These are ordinary local notifications: they are scheduled on your device, by your device, and nothing is sent anywhere to produce them. They are off until you turn them on in Settings, we never ask on first launch, and we do not send “come back and play” messages. Turn them off in Settings or in your device’s notification settings at any time.

Smart bills

On newer iPhones and iPads the game can use Apple Intelligence to write funny bills based on the job you pick. That work happens right on your device, on Apple’s on-device model. The job you typed and the text it writes stay on your device and are not sent to us or to anybody else. Android uses a handwritten set of bills instead.

When you write to us

“Send feedback” in Settings opens a draft in your own mail app. Nothing is sent until you tap send — and if you delete the draft, nothing leaves your phone. The draft comes pre-filled with the app version, your OS version, and your device model (for example “iPhone 17 Pro”), because that is what turns “it broke” into something we can actually fix. There is no name, no account, and no device identifier in it, and you can delete any of that before sending. If you do write, we then have your email address and whatever you wrote, and we keep it only to reply and to fix the thing. Ask us to delete the thread and we will.

What Apple and Google tell us

Because the game is sold through their stores, App Store Connect and the Play Console show us the usual shop-counter numbers: how many downloads and purchases there were, in which countries, on which OS versions, plus ratings, written reviews, and crash counts. It arrives as totals, never as people. We cannot tell who you are from it and there is nothing in it we could link back to your save.

Kids

The game is friendly for all ages and does not knowingly collect info from anyone, including kids. There is no chat, no user-generated content, no sharing of personal info, and no way to talk to other players. The only outside link is the one to your own mail app, on a button an adult would have to tap.

Your choices

There is no account to close and no data of yours for us to hand over or erase, because we do not hold any. Deleting the app removes the save. Game Center and Play Games data is managed inside those accounts, with Apple and Google. And if you have emailed us, ask and we will delete the correspondence.

Changes

If we ever change this policy, we will post the new version here and update the date at the top.

Questions

Reach out any time at grt.feedback@pm.me and we will get back to you.
