What you do

Smash

Hold to swing the hammer. Aim it at the piggies and let them have it.

Collect

Broken piggies rain coins. Sweep them up fast before the run ends.

Pay bills

Rent, power, water, and more keep showing up. Pay them or the Collector skims your cash.

Get debt free

Clear every bill, grab a perk, and roll into the next chapter with bigger everything.

Upgrade

Spend your coins on a big talent tree. Swing faster, hit harder, get luckier.

Dodge the Collector

Let a bill go overdue and the Collector takes a chunk of everything you earn.

No two runs the same

The room, the economy, and the piggies all keep moving on you.

The news happens

STOCK MARKET CRASH. CRYPTO SUMMER. SURPRISE TAX REFUND. Nineteen deadpan headlines roll through and rewrite the economy for a few runs at a time.

Challenge runs

Arm one before you start. Glass Hands halves your swings. Iron Piggies doubles their health. Both pay a lot more, if your hand holds out.

Watch for hats

A piggy in a hat is tougher and holding more. Nineteen hats, so you can spot the payday from across the table.

Day, dusk, night

The apartment follows your clock, so a late session gets dark windows. Seasons roll past outside them as the debt drags on.

Make it your place

Pick your job

Say what you do for a living and every bill re-skins to match. Twenty professions, each with its own set of very specific invoices and a deadpan memo on the bottom.

Decorate the apartment

Four places to live and forty-odd things to put in them. Wall art, table runners, plants, souvenirs, a lava lamp. It all shows up on the table you actually play on.

Nine hammers

From the rusty mallet that came with the apartment to a baseball bat, a clown mallet, and eventually a laser sword. Each one swings differently.

Perks and abilities

Pay a bill on time and a perk sticks with you for the whole chapter. Fourteen of those, plus eleven active abilities you fire off mid-run.

The long haul

Twenty chapters

Clear every bill and the chapter turns over: bigger debts, tougher piggies, and a named reward for getting there. A chapter map opens up at five so you can see the road.

Move Out

Ten chapters in, the lease is done. Move Out wipes the slate for permanent Credit Score, extra swings, and weapons you cannot get any other way.

Achievements and boards

Twenty-three achievements, plus leaderboards for your best day and your fastest run to debt free. All-time and weekly, on Game Center and Play Games.
