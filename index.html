You are broke and rent is due. Good thing your piggy banks are full. Grab a hammer, smash them open, and scoop up the coins before the Collector takes a cut.

Free to start on iPhone, iPad, and Android. One $1.99 unlock, no ads, no timers. · Need help?

What you do

Smash and grab

Hold to swing the hammer, aim, and let them have it. Ceramic cracks, wood splinters, ice shatters — then sweep up the coins before they roll away.

Get debt free

Clear every bill, grab a perk, and roll into the next chapter with bigger everything.

Upgrade

Spend your coins on a big talent tree. Swing faster, hit harder, get luckier.

Dodge the Collector

Let a bill go overdue and the Collector takes a chunk of everything you earn.

No two runs the same

The room, the economy, and the piggies all keep moving on you.

The news happens

STOCK MARKET CRASH. CRYPTO SUMMER. Deadpan headlines keep rolling in and rewriting the economy for a few runs at a time — and those two are the polite ones.

Challenge runs

Arm one before you start. Glass Hands halves your swings. Iron Piggies doubles their health. There are nastier ones further in, and they all pay more — if your hand holds out.

Watch for hats

A piggy in a hat is tougher and holding more. Learn the hats and you can spot the payday from across the table. There are a lot of hats.

Day, dusk, night

The apartment follows your clock, so a late session gets dark windows. Seasons roll past outside them as the debt drags on.

Make it your run

Two players with the same upgrades do not play the same game.

Pick a career

Tech, Trades, Care, Money, Creative. Each one is a real trade-off — Trades puts another piggy on the table and makes every one of them tougher — and you are locked into it until you Move Out. Pick the life, live with it.

Earn a Certification

Go deep enough in a talent tree and you qualify for one. It is a rule change with a catch attached, not a bigger number, and you only ever hold one. Your career brings an option nobody else can take.

Perks and abilities

Pay a bill on time and a perk sticks with you for the whole chapter. There is a long list of them, plus active abilities you fire off mid-run when the table gets loud.

Make it your place

Bills with your name on them

Say what you do for a living — nurse, electrician, barista, whatever it actually is — and every bill re-skins to match, with its own very specific invoices and a deadpan memo on the bottom.

Decorate the apartment

Wall art, a table runner, a plant, a lava lamp — and a lot more where those came from. It all shows up on the table you actually play on. There is more than one place to live, too.

Better hammers

You start with the rusty mallet that came with the apartment. A baseball bat is not far off. What is waiting at the end of the rack you can find out for yourself — each one swings differently.

The long haul

Chapter after chapter

Clear every bill and the chapter turns over: bigger debts, tougher piggies, and a named reward for getting there. Every turn also offers you two rule changes for the chapter ahead — you take one, so no two chapters play the same.

Move Out

Stay in it long enough and the lease is done. Move Out wipes the slate for a permanent Credit Score, weapons you cannot get any other way, and Severance to spend on your Résumé — unlocks that change what the next cycle even has. Then you pick a new career and go again.

Achievements and boards

A long list of achievements to chase, plus leaderboards for your best day and your fastest run to debt free. All-time and weekly, on Game Center and Play Games.

Fair and square

The game is free to start — play First Days, smash a few hundred piggies, and see whether you like it. One purchase of $1.99 unlocks the rest, forever. That is the entire business model.

No ads. No timers, no energy, nothing to wait for. No coin packs — the one purchase is not currency, and nothing you buy swings the hammer for you. No accounts, no trackers, no data collection. And the money in this game is not real and never becomes real.
