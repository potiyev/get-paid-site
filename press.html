Description

One line

Smash piggy banks, pocket the coins, and pay the rent before the Collector does.

Short

You’re broke and the rent is due. Smash piggy banks with a hammer, sweep up the coins, and settle the bill before the Collector takes his cut.

Long

Piggy Smash! Get Paid! takes the universal dread of a due date and makes it something you can hit with a hammer.

Each run is ninety seconds on a kitchen table covered in piggy banks. Aim, charge, swing. Ceramic cracks, wood splinters, ice shatters, and coins go everywhere — sweep them up before they roll away. Then the run ends and the real decision starts: the coins you just earned can buy an upgrade that makes tomorrow better, or they can pay the bill sitting in your mailbox today. They cannot do both.

Miss a due date and the Collector starts skimming a percentage off every run until you settle. Ignore him long enough and you’re evicted.

Bills are written for whatever job you tell the game you have — on newer iPhones, generated on-device by Apple Intelligence, so nothing about you ever leaves the phone. Free to start; one $1.99 purchase unlocks everything. No ads, no timers, no coin packs.

Features

  • Smashing that feels good. Aim, charge, swing. Every kind of piggy bank breaks its own way — ceramic cracks, wood splinters, metal rings, ice shatters — and the later ones do not go down in one hit.
  • Bills written for your actual job. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence they are written on-device by the local model; everywhere else, by hand. Nothing about you leaves the phone either way.
  • The Collector. Miss a due date and he skims a share of every run until you settle up.
  • Four upgrade trees, a rack of hammers to save up for, a long list of perks and active abilities, and world events that rewrite the economy for a few runs at a time.
  • A place of your own. Decorate the apartment as you climb out of debt.
  • The long haul. Debt free, then Overtime, then Move Out — a full prestige for a permanent Credit Score.

Fair and square

  • No ads. Not banners, not interstitials, not rewarded video.
  • No timers, no energy, no waiting to play.
  • No coin packs. There is exactly one purchase and it is not currency.
  • No data collection, no accounts, no trackers.
  • The money in this game is not real and never becomes real.

Please don’t write

Two of these would cause us real problems, so we would rather say it plainly than leave it unsaid:

  • That players earn real money. They do not — it is entirely in-game.
  • That it is a gambling or casino game. There is no wagering mechanic.
  • That it offers financial advice. It does not.
  • That it has ads or rewarded video. It has neither.
