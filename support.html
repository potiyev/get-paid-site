How do I play?

Touch and hold anywhere to swing the hammer. Slide your finger to aim it at the piggies. When a piggy breaks, sweep your finger over the coins to grab them before your run ends.

How do I pay a bill?

Open the Bills screen from the main menu. Tap a bill to pay it. Pay one on time and you may be handed a perk that lasts the rest of the chapter. Ignore your bills and the Collector takes a chunk of what you earn.

What is free, and what does the $1.99 unlock?

First Days — the whole first chapter — is free, and so is everything in it. The unlock opens every chapter after it, the rest of all four talent trees, challenge runs, and Room Decorate. It is one purchase, it is not currency, and there is nothing else to buy in the game, ever.

I paid, but the game still says free

Open Settings in the game and use Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with. The unlock is tied to that account, not to the save file, so it comes back on a new phone the same way. If it still will not take, email us and say roughly when you bought it.

What is a Certification?

A rule change you qualify for by ranking up deep in one of the talent trees — not a bigger number, but a different way the run works, with a downside attached. You hold exactly one at a time. Swapping costs coins, though chapter rewards sometimes hand you a free swap. Your career always brings one option to the pool that nobody with a different career can take.

Can I change my career?

Not until you Move Out. You can re-flavour your bills to a different job within the same career whenever you like, but the career itself is locked for the cycle — that is what makes picking it a decision. Move Out is where you choose again.

What is Move Out?

It is the big reset. Clear every bill through chapter ten and you can leave for a nicer place. You lose your money, upgrades, perks, and Certification, but you keep your lifetime totals and a permanent Credit Score bonus that makes every run after it better. You also come away with Severance to spend on your Résumé, a board of permanent unlocks, and you pick a fresh career on the way out. Some weapons only ever come this way.

Can I play in my language?

The game is fully translated into eleven languages and follows your device by default. To force a different one, open Settings in the game and pick it from the language list.

Why is that piggy wearing a hat?

Because it is tougher than the others. A hat means more health and more money inside, so it is worth the extra swings.

Did I lose my progress?

Your save lives on your device. On iPhone or iPad, make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID and that your app data was part of your backup. On Android, check that backup is on for the app. Deleting the app clears the save, and the two platforms do not share progress.

The sound is not playing

Check that your ringer is on and that the volume is up in the game’s settings. You can turn music and sound on or off any time in the settings screen.
