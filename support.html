How do I play?

Touch and hold anywhere to swing the hammer. Slide your finger to aim it at the piggies. When a piggy breaks, sweep your finger over the coins to grab them before your run ends.

How do I pay a bill?

Open the Bills screen from the main menu. Tap a bill to pay it. Pay one on time and you may be handed a perk that lasts the rest of the chapter. Ignore your bills and the Collector takes a chunk of what you earn.

What is Move Out?

It is the big reset. Clear every bill through chapter ten and you can leave for a nicer place. You lose your money, upgrades, and perks, but you keep your job, your lifetime totals, and a permanent Credit Score bonus that makes every run after it better. Some weapons only ever drop this way.

Why is that piggy wearing a hat?

Because it is tougher than the others. A hat means more health and more money inside, so it is worth the extra swings.

Did I lose my progress?

Your save lives on your device. On iPhone or iPad, make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID and that your app data was part of your backup. On Android, check that backup is on for the app. Deleting the app clears the save, and the two platforms do not share progress.

The sound is not playing

Check that your ringer is on and that the volume is up in the game’s settings. You can turn music and sound on or off any time in the settings screen.
