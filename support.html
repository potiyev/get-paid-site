How do I play?

Touch and hold anywhere to swing the hammer. Slide your finger to aim it at the piggies. When a piggy breaks, sweep your finger over the coins to grab them before your run ends.

How do I pay a bill?

Open the Bills screen from the main menu. Tap a bill to pay it. Pay a bill and you get to pick one of three perks for the chapter. Ignore your bills and the Collector takes a chunk of what you earn.

Did I lose my progress?

Your save lives on your device. If you got a new phone, make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID and that your app data was part of your backup. Deleting the app clears the save.

The sound is not playing

Check that your ringer is on and that the volume is up in the game’s settings. You can turn music and sound on or off any time in the settings screen.

Still stuck?

Email us at grt.feedback@pm.me. Tell us your device and what happened, and we will help you out.
